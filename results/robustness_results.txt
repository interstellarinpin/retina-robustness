KEY FINDINGS
============

1. Corruption-aware training reduced clean accuracy only slightly:
   55.3% -> 53.4% (-1.9 points).

2. The strongest improvement occurred under Gaussian noise:
   - std 10: 23.3% -> 50.5% (+27.2 points)
   - std 20: 16.5% -> 42.7% (+26.2 points)

3. Robust training substantially improved performance under strong blur:
   - radius 4: 15.5% -> 31.1% (+15.5 points)

4. Robust training improved performance under extreme darkness:
   - brightness 0.25: 38.8% -> 54.4% (+15.5 points)

5. Robust training improved low-contrast performance:
   - contrast 0.25: 43.7% -> 58.3% (+14.6 points)

6. Moderate JPEG compression also improved:
   - quality 50: 44.7% -> 53.4% (+8.7 points)
   - quality 30: 40.8% -> 49.5% (+8.7 points)

7. Improvements were not universal.
   Robust training performed worse under:
   - high contrast
   - extreme JPEG compression
   - some clean/mild conditions
